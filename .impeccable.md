## Design Context

### Users

Assam authority, coordination, and radio-broadcast operators working in one operations network. They need to triage deduplicated incidents, assign responders, publish regional updates, prepare approved radio campaigns, transmit those campaigns from one browser, and confirm reception on an independent browser without switching organisational roles or applications.

### Brand Personality

Calm, authoritative, and operational. The interface should inspire public-service trust and urgency without alarmism, decoration, or inflated technical claims.

### Aesthetic Direction

A national public-service operations network with Assam as the current deployment: light-first, high-contrast, structured, and recognizably civic through Public Sans, restrained tricolour accents, operational maps, and formal information hierarchy. Avoid dated portal clutter, generic dashboard card grids, glass effects, neon dark themes, ornamental nationalism, and demo-labelled surfaces.

### Design Principles

1. Present one integrated operational workflow; do not expose a role switch or fragment authority and broadcaster work into separate applications.
2. Make urgent state, ownership, age, accuracy, and next actions visible without relying on colour alone.
3. Preserve protocol truth in the copy: observations are not victims, link receipts are not rescue progress, organisation provisioning is not cryptographic identity proof, and a software decode path is not physical reception evidence.
6. Treat broadcast and receive as station functions within the same product. Device 01 transmits the immutable program; device 02 listens and compares the exact recovered Tier 2 frames.
4. Prefer dense but calm tables, timelines, registers, and work queues over decorative cards; use progressive disclosure for technical evidence.
5. Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard operation and reduced motion, and keep critical controls clear under stress.
7. Make MapLibre the primary spatial control surface: map selection and popup actions must drive the same persisted workflows as the registers beside it.
8. Show Bluetooth mesh, proven internet gateway, radio downlink, and mesh re-entry as distinct transports connected by preserved packet identity.
